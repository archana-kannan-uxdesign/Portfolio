About Me

A closer look at how I got here, how I think about design, and what that’s looked like in practice, from client work to the tools I reach for every day.

Design philosophy

I’ve always thought of design as the place where logic and instinct have to agree. Every project I take on has to hold up two ways: it has to make sense structurally, and it has to feel right to the person using it. That’s the standard I hold my work to: not just if it functions, but if it resonates.

Origin story

Creativity to me was always a form, an expression, of synthesis.

I had this affinity to build, to add my unique vision to the world. From a young age I became immersed in drawing and crafting. My creative inclinations took me in many directions.

I had interests in subjects like math and biology which some may consider unrelated to art, but that experience polished my approach to design.

Art is a creative endeavor, but math taught me that there are hidden patterns that make it captivating; it taught me that even beauty can have structure.

My experience with STEM allowed me to do what I have always done: synthesize, to mix art with science, spontaneity with structure and patterns.

My seemingly unrelated interests uniquely made me gravitate towards User Experience design, where synthesis is the norm and creativity under pre-existing structure is rewarded.
