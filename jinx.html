Jinx

A card deck from Jinx’s chaotic, graffiti-splashed world

A playing-card deck and its packaging, designed around Jinx from Arcane and League of Legends. Spray-paint marks, chaotic graffiti and scrawled symbols capture her unpredictable energy, while a cohesive design language holds every card together as one deck.

Format
Card deck + packaging
Role
Designer, illustrator
Context
SCAD GRDS 348 ‘Studio I: Production and Technique’ course project
Duration
1.5 weeks · 2025
Toolkit
Adobe Photoshop, Procreate

Uncontrollable chaos

01

Concept

I debated three themes before committing: Valorant (through the agent Sage), Cybersigilism and Jinx. I chose Jinx for the room she left to experiment. Her chaotic, expressive world was an invitation to make something unorthodox: a deck that bends the conventions of a playing card instead of following them.

02

Research & moodboard

Research started with the source material. I’ve played League of Legends and watched Arcane, both of which feature Jinx. From there, I gathered visual research on her poses, color schemes and the symbols she’s associated with, from her graffiti tags to the doodles she leaves behind.

03

Color palette

I built the palette from the colors most associated with Jinx: electric magenta, cyan and blues, a violet, two acid greens and a muted plum. Together they’re hyper, sharp and bright, a palette that feels chaotic on purpose and glows like neon against the black of the card backs.

  • #EB00CEPink
  • #06F9FCCyan
  • #00A2DFSky blue
  • #008DFFAzure
  • #8119DFViolet
  • #6CEA2BAcid green
  • #862A58Plum
  • #95E76CLight green

04

Development

Every asset in the deck was hand-drawn in Procreate: the pips, the face cards, the jokers, the card back and every scrawled symbol in between. I wanted the deck to be a completely original work of art, with nothing pulled from stock or traced from elsewhere.
